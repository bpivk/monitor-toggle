<task type="checkpoint:human-verify" gate="blocking">
  <name>Task 2: Rig-validate global hotkey capture, trigger, conflict surfacing, and Settings-race behavior</name>
  <what-built>
    A configurable global hotkey (TRIG-01): a read-only recording textbox in Settings (click to record, next Ctrl/Alt/Shift + key combo captured as e.g. "Ctrl+Alt+R", Escape clears), registered on MainForm's window handle via user32 RegisterHotKey at startup and on Settings-Save, intercepted through a WndProc WM_HOTKEY handler that toggles via ToggleOrchestrator and shows a result balloon toast. Registration failures are surfaced (inline warning in Settings + startup toast), never silently swallowed. The hotkey is unregistered while Settings is open.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
    Run the published exe ON THE RIG with Moza Companion installed. Perform each check and report PASS/FAIL:

    1. CAPTURE UX: Open Settings. The Hotkey field shows "(No hotkey set — click to configure)" in gray. Click it — it turns to the info/highlight color and reads "Press a key combination… (Esc to clear)". Press Ctrl+Alt+R — it captures and displays "Ctrl+Alt+R" and the highlight clears. Click it again, press only Ctrl (bare modifier) and release — it stays in the "Press a key combination…" state (bare modifier not accepted). Click it, press Escape — the field reverts to the gray "(No hotkey set…)" placeholder.

    2. SAVE + PERSIST: Record a hotkey (e.g. Ctrl+Alt+R), click Save Settings. Reopen Settings — the field still shows "Ctrl+Alt+R" (persisted). Fully close and relaunch the exe, reopen Settings — still "Ctrl+Alt+R" (survives restart).

    3. TOGGLE FROM ANYWHERE: With the main window visible, press the hotkey — the mode toggles and a balloon toast reports the new mode + per-step checklist. Now close the window to the tray (X). Bring a game or other app to the foreground and press the hotkey — the mode still toggles and the toast still appears even though the Rig Toggle window is hidden.

    4. CONFLICT SURFACING (the core rig test — Moza Companion RUNNING): With Moza Companion running, in Settings record a combination you believe Moza Companion or other rig software already owns, click Save. Confirm a dedicated inline warning appears next to the Hotkey field ("Could not register hotkey — it may already be in use by another application. …click Save again.") AND the Settings dialog stays open (not silently swallowed, save not lost). If you cannot find a real conflict, note that and instead confirm the warning path by any combination that fails to register. Then close and relaunch the exe with that same conflicting hotkey saved — confirm a startup warning toast appears ("…the configured hotkey could not be registered…") and the app does NOT crash.

    5. SETTINGS-RACE: With a working hotkey configured, open Settings and, while it is open, press the hotkey — confirm nothing toggles (the hotkey is suppressed for the Settings lifetime). Close Settings, press the hotkey again — confirm it toggles normally again (re-registered on close).
  </how-to-verify>
  <resume-signal>Type "GO" if all five checks pass, or list the specific failing check(s) and observed behavior for gap closure.</resume-signal>
