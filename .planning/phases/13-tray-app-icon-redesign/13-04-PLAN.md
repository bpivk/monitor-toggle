<objective>
Fix CR-01 from 13-REVIEW.md: `IconGeometry.cs`'s `DrawNormalIcon`/`DrawRigIcon` call `FillPath` then `DrawPath(outline, path)` on a combined multi-sub-figure `GraphicsPath`. `GraphicsPath.DrawPath` strokes every sub-figure's own boundary independently (it does NOT compute a merged union contour), so every place two sub-shapes touch or overlap gets an extra black seam line, and the current checked-in `normal.ico`/`rig.ico` show visible seam artifacts and interior transparent specks at 16px. Rewrite the outline compositing so only the true union contour is stroked, regenerate the tray icons, and gate the fix behind a raw-pixel interior-artifact diagnostic (the existing byte-level round-trip check only proves the ICO container parses — it did NOT catch this rendering defect). Fold in the two cheap review warnings WR-01 (missing 28/36/40/48px tray frames) and WR-02 (duplicated `256` magic constant).

Purpose: Restore ICON-01 (shape distinguishability — currently undermined by seam clutter) and ICON-02 (16px legibility — currently hurt by transparent holes inside the rig silhouette). This is a targeted bug fix, NOT a redesign: the monitor-vs-wheel motif (D-01), the UI-SPEC geometry fractions, DrawAppIcon, MainForm.cs, and RigToggle.App.csproj are all correct and out of scope.
Output: A rewritten outline-compositing path in IconGeometry.cs, a new pixel diagnostic in Program.cs, and regenerated `normal.ico`/`rig.ico`/`app.ico` that pass both the round-trip and interior-artifact gates, confirmed by a narrow rig re-check.
