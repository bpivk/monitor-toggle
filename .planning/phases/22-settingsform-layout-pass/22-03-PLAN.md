  <what-built>
    The Settings window has been rebuilt from the inside out. It used to be a fixed 828x768 dialog where every control was nailed to a hardcoded pixel coordinate, with everything stacked in a 396px-wide strip down the left half and the right half sitting empty below the two grids.
