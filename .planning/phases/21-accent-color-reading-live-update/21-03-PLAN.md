**Out-of-scope literals that must survive:** `Color.FromArgb(0, 90, 158)` still appears exactly twice in `src/RigToggle.App/ThemeApplier.cs` — on the `grid.DefaultCellStyle.SelectionBackColor` line and the `textBox.BackColor` line, both SettingsForm surfaces outside D-04. It also appears once in `src/RigToggle.IconGen/IconGeometry.cs` as `AppGlassColor` (icon artwork, unrelated). Three total across `src/`.

**The byte-order question this phase must settle numerically.** Plan 01 implemented two deliberately different extractions:
- registry path (`ReadAccentColorFromRegistry`): `r = v & 0xFF`, `g = (v >> 8) & 0xFF`, `b = (v >> 16) & 0xFF` — ABGR, R in the low byte.
- DWM path (`ReadAccentColorFromDwm`): `r = (colorization >> 16) & 0xFF`, `g = (colorization >> 8) & 0xFF`, `b = colorization & 0xFF` — Microsoft's documented `0xAARRGGBB`.
`21-RESEARCH.md` contradicts itself here: its Pattern 1 code matches what was implemented, but its "IMPORTANT correction" paragraph and `21-UI-SPEC.md`'s source-of-truth table row 1 both claim the two paths need identical arithmetic. The tiebreaker used at plan time was `21-RESEARCH.md`'s own cited worked example (`0xffc77e35` → `#357EC7` blue under ABGR, `#C77E35` orange under AARRGGBB). Rig check 3 below settles it against the live machine with real numbers. If it turns out inverted, the fix is swapping two lines in one method — a gap-closure plan, not a redesign.

**Diagnostic evidence available on the rig:** `WindowsThemeProvider.Log()` routes through `Trace.WriteLine` and is persisted to `%APPDATA%\RigToggle\debug.log` only when `AppSettings.EnableDebugLogging` is on. Two log lines matter this phase: the constructor's `Constructed: initial theme resolved to {theme}, accent resolved to {color}` and the handler's `Accent color flip detected: {previous} -> {resolved}`. The flip line is the objective, timestamped separator between "the event fired and the diff worked" and "the repaint applied" — the rig checks below use it to isolate those two failure modes.

**Known open questions this pass resolves:**
- Open Question 2 — is the `AccentColor` registry value present at all on this rig, i.e. is the DWM fallback live code or a defensive branch that never fires? Resolved by rig check 2's `reg query`.
- Assumption A3 — does `SystemEvents.UserPreferenceChanged` fire at all for an accent-only change (no light/dark flip)? Resolved by rig check 5. If it does not fire, `21-CONTEXT.md`'s discretion item authorizes falling back to intercepting `WM_DWMCOLORIZATIONCOLORCHANGED` — but only after this pass proves the message-only approach insufficient, never preemptively.
