<objective>
Make the shipped self-contained win-x64 exe measurably smaller than the recorded v2.1 baseline (49,356,430 bytes) by adding exactly one new, safe, non-trimming MSBuild lever to `src/RigToggle.App/RigToggle.App.csproj`, then prove the result on real rig hardware.

The lever is a manually curated deny-list `<Target>` that removes seven WinForms Design-time and VisualBasic-compatibility assemblies from `@(ResolvedFileToPublish)` before the single-file bundler runs. `24-RESEARCH.md` live-tested it this session: 49,367,342 -> 46,770,879 bytes, a 2,596,463-byte (5.26%) additional reduction on top of v2.0's four existing levers, with zero build warnings and zero source-code references to any of the seven assemblies anywhere in `src/`.

**Decision coverage:**
- **D-01:** lever scope, package-reference swaps in scope — the mandated `dotnet publish` output audit was performed and is closed with a documented **negative** result — `WindowsDisplayAPI` (64 KB) and `NAudio.Wasapi` + `NAudio.Core` (368 KB combined) are already minimal, so this phase makes **zero** `PackageReference` changes. The opportunity the audit surfaced lives in the SDK's own WinForms reference pack, addressed by an MSBuild `<Target>` instead. Task 2 asserts this negative result rather than leaving it implied.
- **D-02:** startup-latency tradeoff acceptable — deliberately unused. No latency-trading lever was needed — the applied lever removes files *before* compression runs, so cold-start decompression work is unchanged or marginally reduced. The allowance stays available for a future phase; nothing here spends it.
- **D-03:** skip the resource-key switch — honored, and extended by the same reasoning to `DebugType=none` (24-RESEARCH.md Pitfall 2: a 167-byte win on the shipped artifact in exchange for losing portable-PDB file:line enrichment in `debug.log` stack traces). Task 2 gates on both staying absent.
- **D-04:** no minimum-savings threshold — honored. Every candidate lever was pursued to a measured conclusion, not dismissed for being small — one qualified, three were ruled out with live evidence (see `24-RESEARCH.md` Common Pitfalls 2 and 4, and State of the Art).

Purpose: PERF-03 — a smaller download and a smaller on-disk footprint for the standalone exe, achieved without touching a single line of application code and without any of the excluded static-analysis-based levers.
Output: one edited `.csproj`, a measured before/after byte-count record, a clean regression audit, and a passed rig verification.
</objective>
