  <name>Task 2: Rig verification of theme behavior (THEME-01..06)</name>
  <what-built>
    Full theme phase: Application.SetColorMode base layer + WindowsThemeProvider live-follow (SystemEvents/AppsUseLightTheme) + DWM Mica/rounded corners + per-control overrides (DataGridView, GroupBox→Panel, txtHotkey) across MainForm, SettingsForm, and MonitorConfirmDialog.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
    First, record the rig's Windows version (Settings > System > About, or `winver`) — this resolves the open question and tells us whether the Windows-10 fallback path is exercised for real. Then run each check in BOTH light and dark mode (toggle via Settings > Personalization > Colors > "Choose your mode" set to Light vs Dark):

    1. LAUNCH-THEME (THEME-01): Start the app normally. MainForm's controls and title bar match the current Windows theme. Open Settings — its controls + title bar match too.
    2. TITLE BAR (THEME-03): In dark mode, both MainForm's and SettingsForm's title bars are dark (not white).
    3. CONTROLS + GRID (THEME-04): In dark mode, every control is legible — no stock white/gray leftovers. Specifically open Settings and confirm the monitor DataGridView (dgvMonitors) has dark cells AND dark column headers (not a white grid), including the two checkbox columns. Click the hotkey box (txtHotkey): it must STAY dark-themed while idle, turn the accent blue while recording, and return to dark-themed after capture — it must NOT flash back to a light/white background at any state.
    4. FLAT STYLING (THEME-05): Buttons on all three forms look flat (no 3D bevel/gradient). The three former GroupBoxes (Monitor / Audio Devices / Application) render as flat bordered panels with captions, with NO layout shift versus before. Drag-and-drop an exe onto the app-path panel still works.
    5. LIVE FLIP — VISIBLE (THEME-02): With MainForm visible, flip Windows Light↔Dark in Settings. MainForm recolors (controls + title bar) WITHOUT restarting the app. Repeat with Settings open — the grid, hotkey box, and title bar all update live.
    6. LIVE FLIP — TRAY HIDDEN (THEME-02 + D-08): Start the app with the `--tray` flag (hidden start). While it is hidden in the tray, flip the Windows theme. Then open the window from the tray — it must appear already in the NEW theme (title bar + controls + grid), proving theme application does not depend on Form_Load.
    7. MONITORCONFIRMDIALOG: Trigger a first toggle so the confirm dialog appears — it is themed (not a light popup) and its two buttons are flat.
    8. WINDOWS-11 CHROME / GRACEFUL DEGRADE (THEME-06): On Windows 11, note rounded corners + a Mica backdrop on both forms. On Windows 10 (if that is the rig), confirm the app simply runs with no crash and no visual glitch (rounded/Mica absent is expected and fine).
    9. NEGATIVE/ACCEPTED: The 4 MessageBox popups staying light (D-01) and the tray right-click menu separator not recoloring after a live flip (D-03) are ACCEPTED known limitations — do not report them as failures.

    Report PASS, or list any check number that failed with what you saw.
