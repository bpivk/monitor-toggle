  <what-built>
    `MainForm` is now a monitor-tile dashboard. One owner-drawn `MonitorTile` per detected monitor sits at the top of the window, showing the ported monitor-silhouette icon (solid-filled in the accent color when the monitor is ON, hollow gray outline when OFF), the monitor's number, and an orange star badge on the primary monitor's tile. Below the tiles: a shared `Identify` button, then the Rig/Normal toggle, then a wider gap, then a small icon-only gear in the bottom-right corner. The window computes its own size from the tile count (288px content floor, wrapping to a second row past four tiles) on every population and hotplug. Clicking a tile — or Tab-focusing it and pressing Space or Enter — enables or disables that one monitor, gated by the existing confirmation dialog when disabling, under the shared exclusive-monitor-access lease. The standalone Monitors panel and both its entry points are deleted.

    Everything below is a property this Linux build environment structurally cannot observe: it has no display hardware, no CCD API, no Windows theme, and no DPI scaling.
