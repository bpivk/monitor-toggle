<objective>
Consolidate the three manual "Check for Updates" entry points down to one (the Help > About dialog), and fix the silent-no-feedback bug that makes that surviving entry point useless: today every manual-check outcome is reported exclusively through `notifyIcon.ShowBalloonTip`, which is a guaranteed no-op whenever `notifyIcon.Visible` is false — and it is false by default, because both `CloseMinimizesToTray` and `MinimizeToTray` default to false.

Purpose: a modal dialog with a button that answers nothing when clicked is broken UX. After this change, the About dialog owns its own always-visible inline status line, and the redundant tray-menu item and Settings button are gone.

Output: a new `UpdateCheckMessageFormatter` in Core (unit-tested, single source of truth for outcome copy), an inline status label on AboutForm fed by the real `UpdateCheckResult`, and the removal of the tray + Settings entry points and their now-dead plumbing.
</objective>
